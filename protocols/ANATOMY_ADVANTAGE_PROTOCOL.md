# Does wiring similarity predict the value of including a person's first scan?

Exploratory follow-up specified before computing these associations. The earlier [pooled-decoder comparison](POOL_AUGMENTATION_PROTOCOL.md) already established the calibration curves in both cohorts. Both cohorts also supplied those earlier results, so the associations here are exploratory in both.

Use NKI–Rockland's odd diffusion directions and Beijing BNU1's whole-scan connectomes, first visit to second visit, analyzed separately. Use the three comparison identities already assigned to each target in the augmentation experiment. For each target/comparison pair, compute the Pearson correlation across all distinct off-diagonal, upper-triangular raw structural edge weights between the target's second scan and (a) the target's own first scan and (b) the comparison person's first scan. Retain absent edges as zeros. The anatomical advantage is (a) minus (b), in raw correlation units. A positive value means the personal first scan more closely resembles the second scan on this measure.

The primary accuracy variable is the difference in mean held-out recall R² between a pool that includes the personal scan and the otherwise identical pool that includes the comparison scan, each donor contributing equally. Use exactly eight labeled target time steps, the smallest positive calibration budget, and average the six calibration draws and ten recall delays. Positive values favor including the personal scan. Within each target average anatomical and accuracy advantages over its three comparison people. Estimate their Spearman correlation across the target participants (one point per person). Use average ranks in the presence of ties. Bootstrap 10,000 paired participant resamples per cohort, seed 20261119, with percentile 95% intervals. Donor assignments, cohorts, fitted decoders and simulated streams are held fixed. Report any undefined correlations without treating them as zero.

Repeat the primary correlation with 25% of the source examples from the added scan. For that allocation, also assess the association within a target across its three comparisons: center anatomical and accuracy advantages by that target's own means, pool the deviations, and compute a Pearson correlation. Resample whole targets for the interval. This secondary estimate describes variation across comparison people for the same target and weights bigger deviations more. Record the mean anatomical advantage and how many participants have a positive mean. Report all analyses in both cohorts irrespective of their signs. An association for these MRI-derived networks and simulated labels is descriptive and cannot identify a mechanism or establish causation.
