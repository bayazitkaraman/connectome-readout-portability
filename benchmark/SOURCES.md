# Data and software sources

| Resource | Access and role |
| --- | --- |
| NKI–Rockland reference and perturbed matrices | Kiar’s archived deterministic structural connectomes. The study uses 25 participants with paired acquisitions and two diffusion-direction halves, plus 20 perturbed runs per acquisition half. Use the pinned original file, identified by its published size and MD5 and the study’s SHA-256. [Zenodo record](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4041549) · [NKI extraction and provenance](../data/nki/provenance.json) |
| NKI visit crosswalk | The public Rockland legacy-to-standard visit map establishes acquisition order. The replay verifies its SHA-256 before extraction. [Public crosswalk](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/enhanced/public_crosswalk_ids_08242023.csv) |
| Beijing Normal University, CoRR BNU1 | Two acquisitions for 57 participants. Lin and colleagues describe the Beijing acquisitions, and Zuo and colleagues describe the CoRR consortium. The study uses public m2g structural connectivity matrices from NeuroData. Fetchers verify the DKT source listing, per-object checksums, and Schaefer atlas and per-edge-file hashes. [Beijing dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2015.56) · [CoRR consortium paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2014.49) · [m2g data documentation](https://docs.neurodata.io/m2g/paper/data.html) · [DKT manifest](../data/replication_candidates/corr/bnu1_source_manifest.json) · [200-parcel manifest](../results/pipeline-robustness/schaefer200_manifest.json) · [300-parcel manifest](../results/pipeline-robustness/schaefer300_manifest.json) |
| Reservoir simulator | Vendored conn2res files retain their upstream BSD license, commit and source-blob hashes. The study fits centered ridge readouts using the included analysis code. [conn2res paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-44900-4) · [Vendored commit record](../vendor/README.md) · [License](../vendor/LICENSE) |

The release distributes derived scores, analysis code, source manifests and protocols. Its retrieval scripts obtain the underlying third-party matrices and crosswalk from their original providers. Use the cited source terms for those materials. The vendor license covers its upstream files.
