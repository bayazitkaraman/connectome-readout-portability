# Origin and interpretation of the NKI diffusion halves

Kiar's source release describes 25 individuals contributing two independently acquired sessions each. Within each acquisition, the authors kept either the odd or even diffusion directions. Each reconstruction used 64 directional volumes and the same nine b0 volumes. Eddy-current correction and alignment were performed on the full diffusion session before downsampling. DKT parcellation was registered to each session. Thus the two halves share an acquisition, baseline images and preprocessing. They use different directional measurements to estimate the structural connections. The analysis here uses the unperturbed deterministic reference runs, with streamline count as edge weight. [Producer's data and methods](https://zenodo.org/records/4041549) · [Author pipeline](https://github.com/gkpapers/2020ImpactOfInstability/blob/9f8345846ad9ef66c3e270bec44f4cf55b72b92d/code/processing/tractography/dipy_tracking.py)

The same-visit comparison measures reproducibility under this specific directional subsampling and reconstruction procedure. Shared preprocessing and acquisition can raise agreement. Separately acquired sessions additionally vary in position, motion, registration, visit interval and possibly underlying anatomy. Reduced directional sampling can lower precision. A within-session estimate consequently serves as a reference without furnishing a mathematical upper bound or identifying a biological cause of the between-visit difference.

## Record-level check

The audit independently read all 100 selected graphs from the original HDF after verifying its published MD5. All matrices matched the analyzed array exactly by participant, legacy visit code and diffusion-direction half. The public crosswalk unambiguously mapped all 25 first visits to baseline (BAS1) and 24 of the 25 second visits. The remaining legacy second visit, NFB2R for A00034854, is retained as recorded. All ten people whose own odd repeat graph was less similar than the mean of their three assigned comparison graphs have explicit second-visit mappings (five BAS2, two FLU1, three TRT). Changing to all 24 other people changes that below-mean count to eight of 25. In the odd-baseline to odd-repeat direction, the own graph ranked first out of 25 for ten people. [Official NKI crosswalk](https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/enhanced/public_crosswalk_ids_08242023.csv)

The flagged participant A00063383's source HDF contains odd and even graphs for CLGA and CLG2R, with official labels BAS1 and TRT. The two halves' whole-matrix upper-triangle correlations were 0.9724 at baseline and 0.9740 at retest. Across visits the four combinations had correlations from 0.0013 to 0.0034. Positive edge counts were 243 and 244 at baseline and 177 and 183 at retest. The participant was their own best anatomical match across halves within each visit and their own lowest-ranked match among 25 candidates in the odd baseline-to-odd repeat comparison. The source records support the pairing in the analysis. Verifying original image identity, registration accuracy and causes of this discrepancy would require acquisition and processing quality information. All participants remain in the analysis.
