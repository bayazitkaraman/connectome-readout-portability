# How far does an individual connectome decoder transfer?

In NKI, a person's two reconstructions from the same diffusion acquisition uniquely identified all 25 people at each visit. A frozen decoder trained on one half outperformed the average separately fitted decoder from the other 24 people for 22 of 25 targets at the first visit and 24 of 25 at the second. The transfer advantage was much smaller across independent visits. Even within an acquisition the transferred readout recovered only part of the recall achievable by direct fitting on the target graph.

## Same-person decoder, zero target labels

The primary comparison trains each readout on odd directions and tests on even directions. Each row's own and stranger models predict the same four held-out signals for each target. For each target/stranger pair, the two decoders share a transfer penalty selected using the other 23 people's pairs. R² is averaged over ten simulated recall delays. The uncertainty intervals are paired 95% participant bootstrap intervals conditional on fitted decoders, donors and signals.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Visit 1 odd → visit 1 even | 25/25 | 0.119 | −0.018 | +0.137 [0.080, 0.189] | 22/25 |
| Visit 2 odd → visit 2 even | 25/25 | 0.242 | −0.095 | +0.337 [0.276, 0.399] | 24/25 |
| Visit 1 odd → visit 2 even | 10/25 | 0.047 | 0.026 | +0.022 [−0.018, 0.059] | 17/25 |
| Visit 2 odd → visit 1 even | 11/25 | 0.059 | 0.032 | +0.027 [0.005, 0.048] | 15/25 |

With the **same target even half** and identical test inputs, the personal decoder scored 0.060 R² higher when its source came from the same acquisition for visit 1 [0.002, 0.113], and 0.194 higher for visit 2 [0.129, 0.254]. Reusing the exact cross-visit penalty for both sources gave advantages of 0.094 [0.063, 0.127] and 0.129 [0.085, 0.177], respectively. Reversing the halves reproduced the within-acquisition structural retrieval (25/25 at both visits) and personal decoder advantages (24/25 and 25/25). A target-trained, separately validated decoder reached 0.966 and 0.949 on the two even targets. The two visits are repeated measurements of 25 people. **Interpretation.** The source release divided 128 diffusion directions into odd and even sets of 64. Both sets used the same nine baseline volumes and shared preprocessing within the acquisition. This is a within-acquisition repeatability check. It cannot isolate biological change from differences in acquisition, motion, registration or tractography between visits. Its scores are an empirical reference, with no formal upper-bound property. The same person can be anatomically identifiable while a readout trained on a different reconstruction still loses substantial task accuracy. [Kiar et al., source methods](https://zenodo.org/records/4041549)

## Cross-visit result for the paper

With zero labels from an unseen target scan, the original transfer-tuned single-person decoder scored 0.043 in NKI versus 0.024 for a decoder from another person, mean paired gain +0.019 [−0.027, 0.061]. In Beijing the respective scores were 0.153 and 0.103, gain +0.049 [0.025, 0.070]. Each other person supplied a separately fitted decoder. For context, training directly on the target with 2,352 labeled simulated examples gave mean R² of 0.952 and 0.836. The number of target examples and type of evaluation should accompany every reference value.

In NKI, the wiring-similarity advantage tracked the zero-label accuracy gain (Spearman ρ = 0.762 [0.485, 0.904]) when both contrasts used all 24 other participants. Beijing's corresponding estimate was 0.243 [−0.022, 0.481]. Within NKI, a descriptive median split on the correlation between the two personal scans reproduces gains of +0.079 for the upper 13 and −0.046 for the lower 12. These groups were selected after examining the data. The correlation is the more informative summary. At eight labels, the equal-share *pooled augmentation* gain changed from +0.00530 to −0.00063 after removing the two largest observed gains. That deletion illustrates the sensitivity of the mean to individual outcomes. It does not justify excluding either participant. The single-source and pooled-augmentation comparisons ask different questions. **Weak pairs.** Ten NKI own scan pairs fell below the mean similarity to the three originally assigned comparators. Eight fell below the all-24 average, while ten were the unique closest structural match across the original odd first-to-odd second visits. In Beijing three own pairs fell below the comparison mean under either donor definition. For the flagged NKI participant, the odd-to-odd cross-visit correlation was 0.0014 (rank 25/25), versus 0.972 and 0.974 between halves within their first and second acquisitions (rank 1/25 both times). The released session codes for this case agree with the public BAS1 and TRT mapping, and all ten of the weak three-comparator cases have mapped visits. This identifies a discrepancy in reconstructed wiring across visits. The derivative records alone cannot adjudicate the underlying image identity or its cause. We retained everyone.

## Why the calibration tie is secondary

At the original cohort thresholds, 48 labels in NKI and 64 in Beijing, the predictions from the two equal-donor augmented decoders had practically converged. We reconstructed their predictions from the original source fits and archived penalty choices. Across the target/other-person pairs and six calibration draws, their mean root-mean-square prediction differences were **0.000058 and 0.000027 target standard deviations**, respectively. At zero labels these differences were **0.0478 and 0.0236**. Reconstructed R² scores agreed with the archived scores to within 5 × 10⁻¹⁶. The trained predictions retained small differences at the thresholds. A synthetic personal signal saved eight examples when the added scan supplied 25% of the source data, demonstrating that a threshold tie is possible without being inevitable. [Prediction-level comparisons](prediction_convergence.csv) · [Positive control](../density-controls/positive-control/README.md)

For the equal-share *pooled scan-swap* in NKI, the anatomy-gain rank correlations were 0.718 before calibration and 0.720 after eight labels. The eight-label scores were lower on average in all three pooled arms in this comparison. Zero labels directly measures what transferred and is a sensible anchor. This is a revised interpretation following the review. The original prespecified sample-savings result remains zero on its tested grid.

We also tested the single-person decoder comparison itself at 32 target labels, using every other donor and six paired calibration draws. NKI gave mean R² of 0.4369 for the personal source and 0.4384 for the other sources, a difference of −0.0015 [−0.0035, +0.00004]. Beijing gave 0.2965 and 0.3004, a difference of −0.0039 [−0.0072, −0.0007]. The positive mean personal advantage seen before calibration had disappeared in both cohorts by this budget.
