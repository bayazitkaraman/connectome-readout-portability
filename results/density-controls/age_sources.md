# Baseline ages of the actual repeat-scan participants

All 25 NKI participants had a matching, session-specific baseline diffusion MRI record. Their reported ages ranged from **16 to 69 years** (median 17, interquartile range 16 to 28, mean 25.48, sample SD 15.52). Fifteen were age 16 or 17. Four were within the Beijing cohort's 19 to 30 year age range. The original Beijing acquisition paper reports an age range of **19 to 30 years**, a mean of 23.05 and an SD of 2.29 for its 57 participants. The two cohorts differ in age composition. These summaries alone cannot determine whether age explains the anatomy–accuracy association. [NKI imaging documentation](https://rocklandsample.org/accessing-the-neuroimaging-data-releases) · [Original Beijing acquisition](https://www.nature.com/articles/sdata201556)

The structural matrices come from Kiar and colleagues' 25-person NKI–Rockland analysis. We matched the identifiers in the archived matrices to **BAS1** in the Enhanced NKI–Rockland imaging index. The index has one row per file. Filtering for the baseline diffusion NIfTI path ending in `_dwi.nii.gz` gave exactly one age-bearing row for each of the 25 participants. The corresponding 25 baseline T1-weighted NIfTI records independently returned the same 25 ages. All six entries in each person's baseline diffusion directory also agreed on age. Other file types include inconsistent age entries, so pooling ages across file types would give a misleading range. A CoRR site label or an age range for the entire NKI study cannot determine the ages of these particular people. [Kiar et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8559953/) · [Official public imaging index](https://rocklandsample.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/aws_links.csv)

For reproduction, use the subject identifiers from `data/nki/nki_reference.npz`. Verify that the first row of `standard_session_labels` is BAS1 for all 25, select the matching subject IDs and BAS1 diffusion image rows in the linked index, and summarize its numeric `age` field. The SHA-256 of the downloaded imaging index was `3ceae2b45965bec28b0669243659a39946d473e9f6acd97d6a5023ded0a14ffc`. Only aggregated demographics are reported here.
