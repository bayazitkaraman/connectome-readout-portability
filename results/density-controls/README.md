# Does scan density explain the NKI result?

The review correctly identified edge count and the reuse of comparison people as two possible explanations. The eight-example NKI association persisted after controls for both. Beijing's association was weak under the same tests. These results concern recall of simulated signals on MRI-derived networks, with 25 independent target people in NKI and 57 in Beijing.

## Direct checks on the explanation

The outcome is the personal first scan's held-out R² advantage over a different person's first scan when each is added to the same pooled readout. The predictor is the personal first scan's advantage in structural similarity to the target's second scan. Primary analyses use an equal source contribution per donor and eight target examples.

For the last row, one of the three assigned people supplies the anatomical comparison and the other two supply the accuracy comparisons. We rotate these roles and average the three rank correlations. Three sampled donors limit estimates of donor-population uncertainty. **Per-scan counts.** There were 3,403 possible distinct undirected edges. NKI ranged from 177 to 626 positive edges across its 50 scans, with a median of 418.5. Beijing ranged from 1,291 to 2,155 across its 114 scans, with a median of 1,567.5. Absolute-agreement scan-rescan ICC was 0.44 in NKI [0.05, 0.70] and 0.59 in Beijing [0.29, 0.78]. Median absolute within-person change was 77 edges in each cohort. The earlier PDF gave no per-scan counts. The earlier archive provided density fractions from which integer counts can be recovered. [One row per scan](observational/scan_counts.csv) · [Agreement](observational/test_retest.csv)

**Matched-pair regression.** In the 75 NKI comparisons, a 0.10 increase in anatomical similarity advantage corresponded to +0.00676 R² [0.00405, 0.00947] after adjusting for density mismatch with crossed target and donor random intercepts. Before adjustment the estimate was +0.00687. The adjusted slope in 171 Beijing pairs was +0.00106 [−0.00061, 0.00274]. A separate regression with target/donor bootstrap weights also gave a positive adjusted NKI slope. These estimates use the reviewer's mismatch definition: the absolute target-to-other density gap minus the absolute target-to-own gap. [Model coefficients and diagnostics](observational/crossed_models.csv)

**Which edges exist and how much they weigh.** The original similarity uses all upper-triangle weights, including zeros. Shared absences and the arrangement of positive weights both affect this correlation. As a check, we fixed each scan's edge support and weight distribution, then calculated the expected Pearson correlation if the positive weights were randomly assigned to present edges independently in each scan. In NKI the mean own-to-repeat correlation fell from 0.522 to an expected 0.132. In Beijing it fell from 0.846 to 0.045. Nevertheless, the ranks of NKI participants' similarity advantages based on the support-preserving expectation tracked the original ranks closely (ρ = 0.904). Edge support can therefore carry information about *who ranks higher* even when positive-weight placement contributes substantially to the correlation magnitude. An additional NKI regression favored Jaccard overlap of present edges over shared-edge weight correlation, but the interval for the Jaccard coefficient included zero under a two-way bootstrap. A single edge count cannot capture which edges overlap. [Support-preserving expectations](mechanisms/support_weight_permutation_means.csv) · [Support and weight models](observational/crossed_models.csv)

**What normalization does.** The simulation zeroes the matrix diagonal, scales the other weights by 0.90 divided by the largest absolute eigenvalue, and applies tanh. The pooled readout centers features and uses a variance-scaled ridge penalty. Fixing spectral radius allows state amplitudes to vary across people. With identical stimulation, however, scan density correlated only 0.057 and 0.132 with global readout RMS at the two NKI visits. The rank correlation of the personal scan's amplitude-mismatch advantage with its eight-example accuracy gain was 0.078 [−0.420, 0.513]. Regional activity patterns, topology and scan quality remain possible sources of variation. [Readout amplitudes](mechanisms/scan_amplitudes.csv) · [Matched amplitude estimates](mechanisms/correlations.csv)

**Thresholding and rewiring.** We kept the 177 strongest positive edges in every NKI scan and 1,291 in every Beijing scan. A second fixed tie order gave correlations of 0.745 and 0.156, respectively. The fully calibrated target task remained solvable, with mean R² of 0.955 in NKI and 0.836 in Beijing. This trimming alters topology and retains median 90.7% and 99.96% of original total weight, respectively. A universal intersection mask has just eight edges in NKI. Rewiring only the personal candidate would treat the two arms differently. Persistence after such a substitution could reflect the degree sequence, strengths, input locations or other preserved features. We excluded that one-sided intervention from the evidence for density. [Thresholded-network checks](equal-density/README.md)

## Cohort and calibration interpretation

The 25 actual NKI baseline diffusion records span ages 16 to 69, with a median of 17. Fifteen participants were 16 or 17, and four fell within Beijing's age range of 19 to 30. We matched the public records by participant, scan type and visit. The samples and reconstruction pipelines differ, so age or scan quality cannot be identified as the reason for the cohort contrast. [Age sources and verification](age_sources.md)

The between-person SD of the anatomical advantage was 0.208 in NKI and 0.049 in Beijing. Keeping only NKI participants inside the observed Beijing range reduced NKI's correlation to 0.527 with 10 people. The central 90% of Beijing's range left five NKI people and a correlation of 0.00. The cohort slope contrast was positive under the mixed model, while the marginal two-way bootstrap interval for the slope difference crossed zero. The overlap gives limited information about whether the underlying slopes differ. [Range-restricted results](observational/restricted_range.csv) · [Slope comparisons](observational/slope_difference.csv)

The original threshold remains an outcome for sample savings. Both cohorts yielded the same threshold with either starting pool, and all five original configurations tied at the cohort level. Earlier continuous scores can detect smaller differences. With equally weighted donors the mean pooled own-minus-other R² gain at zero target labels was +0.00739 in NKI and +0.00195 in Beijing. The normalized area under the gain curve from 0 to 32 target examples was +0.00367 [−0.00115, 0.01078] and +0.00051 [−0.00117, 0.00189]. With a 25% contribution from the added scan these area estimates were +0.00933 [−0.00081, 0.02159] and +0.00539 [0.00167, 0.00920]. The previously reported Beijing +0.00232 was the equally weighted average across six budgets under the 25% source allocation. The comparable NKI value was +0.00397 [−0.00037, 0.00948]. These new early-area comparisons are exploratory. At large budgets R² differences approach numerical zero, so a sizeable rank correlation can coexist with a negligible gain. [All six budgets, zero and early area](observational/budget_curves.csv)

For the separate zero-label comparison of two *single-person* decoders, we tested each person's first scan against every other first scan within the cohort. Both candidate decoders used the same transfer penalty fitted without either participant. The own-minus-other R² gain on fresh test sequences averaged +0.0191 [−0.0272, +0.0612] in NKI and +0.0492 [+0.0250, +0.0696] in Beijing. This covers all 600 and 3,192 directed single-donor comparisons. It answers which single person's decoder to use before calibration. The pooled scan-swap answers what a personal scan contributes when a population model is already available. [All-donor frozen comparison](frozen/summary.csv)

## Does the pipeline detect an effect when one is present?

We evaluated a synthetic positive control with six of ten delay channels carrying an assigned personal sign that was stable across two visits. With the planted component disabled, own and other tied on every draw. With the component enabled and equal source shares, early R² showed a gain of 0.0554 at zero labels and 0.0232 at eight, yet both models first crossed R² of 0.50 at eight examples. With a 25% source share the synthetic personal model did save eight examples. This supports reporting both early accuracy and threshold savings. The simulation measures sensitivity to a known large effect. It gives no power estimate for the much smaller effects in human-derived graphs. [Synthetic design and results](positive-control/README.md)

On the measured graphs, we also checked radii 0.70, 0.90 and 1.00 and three multipliers of donor-validated source ridge (0.1, 1 and 10), leaving the target adaptation procedure fixed. At the validated ridge the eight-label NKI correlations were 0.735, 0.720 and 0.652, respectively. Beijing's were 0.269, 0.151 and 0.057. Across the nine settings per cohort the ranges were 0.314–0.735 and 0.057–0.269. Individual accuracy estimates varied with radius and regularization. All settings are reported without post hoc selection. [NKI sweep](regime/nki/summary.csv) · [Beijing sweep](regime/bnu1/summary.csv)

**Conclusion.** NKI's eight-example result survived adjustment for total connection count and a refit with exactly equal counts. The estimate was smaller with disjoint comparison people and remained positive. The split also reduces the number of comparisons averaged on each axis. Beijing provided little evidence of a comparably strong association. Which connections recur, their weights, and reconstruction quality remain plausible contributors. Current results give little basis for treating density alone as a substitute for a personal scan, inferring a causal effect of age, or claiming that the R² threshold rules out all useful personal information.
