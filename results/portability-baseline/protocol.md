# Unlabeled parcel alignment on a repeat scan

Analysis specification recorded before scoring the alignment. This is a follow-up to the completed portability analysis. It uses the existing participants, between-visit predictions and cohort transfer penalties.

- **Population and task.** First to second visit within participant, 25 NKI participants (odd-direction halves) and 57 Beijing participants (whole scans). Predict the preceding ten simulated inputs with the existing linear ridge decoder. Hold the reservoir, simulated task, number of source training states (2,352), readout and cohort penalty fixed (NKI 10, Beijing 1).
- **Target-state budget.** For each participant, generate one additional target-graph calibration stream. Discard the 200-step warm-up, retain 2,352 consecutive observations of every readout coordinate, and use only these unlabeled reservoir states. The random input stream uses the existing seed sequence with role 219, repeat 0 and the target's visit-two stream ID. This role is distinct from source training, validation and all four evaluation streams. The two coordinate moments at the source come from the usual first-visit training data.
- **Transformation.** For each corresponding parcel, subtract its target calibration mean, multiply by its source training population SD divided by its target calibration population SD, and add its source training mean. If the parcel is constant in either reference series, map its held-out values to its source training mean. Use a zero-range check for constant coordinates. Apply the resulting fixed transformation to new target states. Preserve coefficients and intercept. There is one alignment method, one budget and one penalty per cohort.
- **Comparators and endpoint.** Score the unchanged source, the untouched repeat, and the aligned repeat on the same four independently seeded 512-row evaluation streams (role 61). The primary endpoint is each participant's difference between aligned and untouched mean delay-specific R². Compute cohort means and 95% paired percentile intervals by resampling participants 10,000 times (seed 20261120). Report positive scores and within-person improvements as counts. For each repeat condition, retained performance is its cohort mean divided by the cohort mean source score.
- **Verification and interpretation.** Verify source and untouched-repeat scores against the archived matched-penalty visit rows. Target labels are used only for held-out scoring. The aligned condition consumes 2,352 extra unlabeled target states, beyond the data available to the original frozen-transfer condition. The intervals condition on the participants' realized calibration and evaluation streams.
